Evaluating Language Model Assistance for Flow Control in Software-Defined Networks

The audited study compares 14 configurations on 12 paired synthetic traces. SELF-X averages 60.54 ms delay, 24.88% loss, and 6.621 Mbps goodput. Mean delay falls by 25.15% versus static forwarding and 15.65% versus the same stack at a 5 s decision cadence. DPP alone averages 60.42 ms. The paired delay difference for the full stack versus DPP alone has a Holm-adjusted p-value of 0.0745.

Audited system model

Both simulated paths serve up to 10 Mbps and each buffer holds up to 2 Mbit. The primary and alternate paths have 2 ms and 12 ms propagation delays. Background demand stays on primary. The controller can keep the bulk aggregate on primary, reroute it, or meter it on primary.

Each 100 ms slot updates backlog according to admitted traffic and service. The simulator clips backlog to the buffer and counts overflow and metered drops separately. The action guard checks projected alternate utilization and permissible meter rates.

The controller scores eligible actions using predicted queue drift and a weighted penalty. The frozen LoRA action prior refreshes every 5 s, and language model governance updates the objective every 30 s. The study evaluates synthetic traffic on this fluid model.
